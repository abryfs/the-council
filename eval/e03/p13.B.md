## Relevant failure patterns (apply these; do not quote them back)

- **Metaphor Fusion** [warn] — A product that fuses several familiar categories inherits none of their mental models, so a new user has no prior to fall back on and no one-sentence description to pass along; adoption then depends on formal explanation, which does not travel. TRIGGER: A spec describes the surface by joining categories with "and" ("it's a doc and a chat and a board"), unifies two existing product surfaces into one, or cannot state what the thing is in one sentence a non-user would repeat.
- **Positioning To The Wrong Frame** [warn] — The category you claim determines which competitors the buyer mentally lines you up against and which features they treat as table stakes. Claim a broad, incumbent-owned category and your genuine differentiator is scored as a minor feature; claim the frame where that differentiator is the whole point and the same product becomes the obvious choice. TRIGGER: Copy or a spec that names a broad established category ("CRM", "project management", "observability", "coordination layer") without naming the specific buyer and the specific alternative they'd otherwise use.
