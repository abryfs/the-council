## Candidates (8, ranked by lexical match)

- **Guessing The Bottleneck** [warn] — Intuition about performance is trained on the code you read, while cost lives in behavior you cannot see — allocation, cache misses, scheduler and GC intervals, tail latency. Optimizing before profiling spends effort on the wrong axis and hides the real cause behind a changed baseline. TRIGGER: A diff or plan doing performance work — caching, batching, async, index, language/runtime swap — with no profile, benchmark, or production measurement cited, or a claim that something "should be faster".
- **Error Text From Inside** [warn] — A message written from the system's state names a condition the user has no model for and no action attached to; it is technically accurate and operationally useless, so the user's only remaining move is to ask a human or abandon the task. TRIGGER: A diff adds or changes user-visible error, empty, or status copy that names an internal condition (state names, error codes, subsystem names, exception text) instead of what failed plus one concrete action the user can take.
- **Preserving The Shape** [warn] — When a change is bolted onto an existing structure specifically to avoid disturbing it — because touching it would trigger a migration, recertification, or retraining cost — the new behavior inherits constraints it was never designed for, and the reason to avoid them becomes a reason not to fix them. TRIGGER: A change explicitly scoped to avoid touching an existing schema, interface, migration, or retraining/re-certification cost — phrases like "without changing the existing", "to avoid a migration", "keep it backward compatible" driving the design rather than following it.
- **Curated Demo Data** [warn] — Designs validated on hand-picked content encode assumptions about length, density, freshness, and correctness that real data violates on day one; the demo proves the layout, not the product, and the gap surfaces publicly at launch scale. TRIGGER: A design, screenshot, or demo uses seeded, curated, or short sample data; or a spec has no defined empty state, long-string, stale-data, error, and high-volume case for the surface being built.
- **Unlabeled Working Surface** [warn] — Surfaces "no user sees" — shell prompts, admin panels, log output, internal dashboards — are used by humans under time pressure, and identical-looking contexts there produce identity confusion at the exact moment stakes are highest. Unseen does not mean unused; it means untested. TRIGGER: Work touches an internal-only surface — CLI output, shell/SSH tooling, admin console, staging vs prod environment, log lines, migration scripts — and the change is justified as "internal, no design needed."
- **Accessibility As Post-Ship Bug** [block] — Accessibility is decided by structure — semantics, focus order, names, contrast — which is fixed at the moment the component is built; deferring it converts a design constraint into a rewrite, and in many jurisdictions into legal exposure that no backlog priority can defer. TRIGGER: A spec or diff ships an interactive component with a11y noted as follow-up, or introduces custom interactive markup (div-as-button, custom modal, canvas UI, drag-and-drop) with no keyboard path, focus management, or accessible name.
- **Undifferentiated Price War** [warn] — Competing on a dimension your rival can copy within a week converts the whole category's margin into customer subsidy, and both sides must keep spending to hold position they can never win. The winner is whoever has more capital, not whoever built the better product. TRIGGER: The plan competes on price, free-tier size, limits, discounting, or speed of copying a feature, in a market where a funded competitor can match the move immediately.
- **Wartime Frame In Peacetime** [warn] — Consensus-building, broad exploration, and process are correct when the constraint is opportunity and wrong when the constraint is survival — and vice versa. Teams keep whichever mode they last succeeded in, so the mismatch persists exactly through the period where it is most expensive. TRIGGER: An existential condition is stated (runway, a competitor shipping the core feature, a broken primary flow, zero users) and the proposed response is a broad multi-track plan, a process change, or a round of alignment.
