## Candidates (8, ranked by lexical match)

- **Premature Scaling** [block] — Capacity built ahead of demand converts fixed costs into a deadline: the burn rate now sets how long you have to find the demand, rather than the demand setting how much you build. Because the capacity is sunk, the team also loses the option to change the shape of the business when the early signal comes back wrong. TRIGGER: A plan that adds headcount, geographies, warehouses, regions, or provisioned infrastructure capacity on a schedule rather than in response to a measured demand signal; "build it so it scales to N" before N/100 exists.
- **Skipping The Unscalable** [warn] — Automating the first-user experience before doing it by hand encodes your guesses about what users need into software, and the automation then hides the disconfirming evidence. Manual onboarding is not inefficiency; it is the only instrument fine-grained enough to find the problem that the metrics summarize away. TRIGGER: A plan to build self-serve signup, an onboarding wizard, an email drip, an in-app tour, or a help center before roughly ten users have been onboarded by a founder in person.
- **Premature Platformization** [block] — A protocol, plugin API, or generality invented before one concrete use is in production is designed against imagined consumers, so it optimizes for flexibility no one needs while missing the constraint the first real user would have imposed. It also raises the cost of every subsequent change, because generality is what you promise not to break. TRIGGER: A spec introduces a protocol, plugin API, extension point, federation model, or abstraction justified by future third parties ('so others can build on it', 'generic enough for N cases') before one concrete case is in production.
- **One-Way Door, Casual** [warn] — Decisions get processed at the weight of their immediate cost, not their reversibility, so a cheap-to-decline strategic option receives less scrutiny than an expensive-to-build feature. The irreversibility only becomes visible after the window that would have let you act on it has closed. TRIGGER: A decision that forecloses a future option (a partnership declined, a public API committed, a naming/positioning lock, a data model that cannot be migrated) is being made in the same pass as routine work, with no note distinguishing it.
- **One-Way Door Misread** [block] — Anything external parties can build against — a public API, tool name, URL scheme, event type, wire field, file format — becomes unrevocable the moment someone depends on it, because the cost of removal is paid by them and shows up as broken trust rather than a rollback. Such decisions get made at the speed of a reversible one because the diff looks small. TRIGGER: A diff or spec publishes something external parties will depend on: a public API endpoint, an MCP or CLI tool name, an event type or enum value, a URL scheme, a config key, an on-disk or wire format.
- **Beautiful Prototype Bias** [note] — Perceived usability tracks aesthetics more tightly than actual usability, so a polished mock or prototype scores well in review and testing regardless of whether the interaction works; the visual finish suppresses exactly the criticism the review exists to surface. TRIGGER: A design is approved from a high-fidelity mock, Figma prototype, or rendered screenshot; or usability testing is run on a visually finished build without a low-fidelity or degraded-data comparison.
- **Adding People To A Late Thing** [warn] — New people on a late project consume the time of the people who know the system, and communication paths grow quadratically with headcount, so the near-term effect on a component with tight internal coupling is negative. Partitionable work is the exception, and the work that is late is rarely the partitionable kind. TRIGGER: A slipping deliverable is answered by assigning more engineers, more parallel agents, or more concurrent sessions to the same component or file set, rather than by cutting scope.
- **Product Without Distribution** [block] — Product quality and reach are independent variables, and quality has no mechanism to produce reach on its own — a plan composed entirely of product work therefore converges on an excellent thing nobody can find, while the burn clock runs at the same rate either way. TRIGGER: A plan, sprint, or milestone is entirely product surface — polish, refactor, design review, quality bar — with no line item for how anyone discovers, installs, or is onboarded to it, or the distribution path is known to 404.
